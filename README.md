# GT IPTV Player Pro 1.8.0-r0

★ **DVB EPG:** Programme guide for satellite channels and manual EPG import. (Rebuilt.)

★ **Reordering settings:** Move rows with the Yellow button and save the order permanently.

★ **Matches page:** Live, completed and upcoming matches, with scores, team logos and filters.

★ **YouTube match highlights:** Search for highlights of completed matches with team and date verification.

★ **LiveOnSat tab:** View the channels broadcasting each match, with a saved country selection.

★ **Movie and series trailers:** Search for and play official trailers with the PLAY button; prioritize trailers for new episodes of series.

★ **Moving Live TV channels:** Move channels with the Yellow button in Xtream and Stalker; press OK to save or EXIT to cancel.

★ **Category sorting:** **A–Z, Z–A and Original** options for Xtream and Stalker.

★ **Movie and series sorting:** **A–Z, Z–A, Recently Added, Year and Original**, including All Movies and All Series.

★ **Live TV recording with REC:** Save an HDD/USB recording folder, start or stop recordings, and show a blinking REC indicator in the infobar.

★ **Overall theme design:** Redesigned and improved.

★ **Updates:** Added remote software updates.

Signed stable updates: Settings > Software update. Publishing: [UPDATING.md](UPDATING.md).
